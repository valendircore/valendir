The problem

The risk is not that AI is wrong.
It is that nobody can prove why it was trusted.

Your teams will use AI for decisions that touch safety, law, money, vehicles, customers, and reputation. The missing layer is not better AI — it is controlled reliance on AI.

Risk 01

Unsupported claims

A model says a design, control, policy, or vehicle behavior is safe. No one knows which facts made that answer stand — or whether they were facts at all.

Risk 02

Evidence laundering

Model opinion gets treated as a test result, legal source, simulation, or engineering review. Confidence detaches from proof. Institutions ship answers they cannot defend.

Risk 03

No replay

When a regulator, safety board, or executive asks why the AI recommendation was relied on, the trail is missing. There is no record, no owner, no stop reason — only the output.

Clearance runtime

Model output is not a decision.
Clearance is.

Valendir does not pretend to make LLMs deterministic. It makes their use controlled. The runtime decides whether the output is clean enough to rely on and writes down exactly why — every time, append-only.

Valendir is not a law firm or certification body. It is the technical evidence layer that helps teams prepare, preserve, and reuse proof behind AI decisions.

EU AI Act

High-risk AI teams need risk management, documentation, transparency, human oversight, robustness, and records as part of the decision flow — not a retrospective slide deck.

  • Maps each answer to risk class, user role, workflow, and evidence status.
  • Captures human approval and reasons for release, hold, or escalation.
  • Keeps technical decision records current as AI outputs evolve.
EU AI Act overview →

ISO 26262

Vehicle programs already understand safety cases, hazards, verification, validation, and traceability. Valendir brings that discipline to AI-assisted analysis affecting road-vehicle engineering decisions.

  • Connects AI claims to requirements, hazards, assumptions, and review owners.
  • Separates model judgment from test evidence, simulation, and engineering signoff.
  • Preserves traceable records for safety and quality teams at claim level.
ISO 26262 overview →

ISO/PAS 8800

For road vehicles using AI, safety arguments must be explicit, reviewable, and replayable. Valendir operationalizes the AI safety argument at the moment the recommendation is made.

  • Tracks AI insufficiencies, performance assumptions, and operating boundaries.
  • Captures V&V evidence at claim level, not just document level.
  • Supports continuous monitoring and structured reopening of AI decisions.

Objections

Straight answers
for serious buyers.

Does Valendir make LLMs deterministic?

No. It makes their use bounded. The model can still vary. Valendir controls what is trusted, logged, escalated, and approved before the institution acts on the output.

Is this a governance dashboard?

No. Dashboards observe. Valendir enforces clearance before an AI recommendation enters a high-impact workflow. Observation without enforcement is documentation of risk, not control of it.

Is this only for automotive?

No. Automotive is a strong proof market because safety teams already understand traceability and evidence. The control layer applies anywhere AI recommendations affect safety, law, money, or reputation.

Can this replace legal or certification work?

No. It creates the technical evidence layer that legal, safety, quality, and certification teams need in order to review, sign off, or certify. Valendir is infrastructure — not a substitute for expert judgment.

Where does Valendir run?

On your infrastructure or ours. Air-gap and on-prem deployments can be supported. No AI recommendation payload leaves your environment unless you choose a managed option with explicit data processing terms.

How long to see a first clearance packet?

A Workflow Discovery engagement produces real packets in two weeks. A Protected Workflow Pilot has clearance running against live AI output within the first week. There is no multi-month setup phase.
