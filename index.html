First facts

Not a dashboard.
A commercial authorization primitive.

Valendir is a finite state machine for autonomous commerce. It moves a clearance from intent to release only when identity, delegated authority, proof, policy, and acceptance conditions are satisfied.

Pending → Identified → Mandate → Proof → Accepted → Released.

Each transition is recorded in a tamper-evident audit chain. Each high-consequence release can be represented by a signed attestation packet that downstream systems can enforce.

01

Non-custodial by design. Valendir does not need to hold funds, bank details, contracts, or invoice bodies. It holds state, hashes, nonces, signatures, and release readiness.

02

Agent-bound cryptographic identity. Agents sign nonce-scoped actions with Ed25519 keys. This is IAM for autonomous systems, not repurposed human SSO.

03

Tamper-evident continuity. Audit entries hash their predecessors, creating cryptographic continuity across state transitions, proofs, disputes, and releases.

04

Reputation as access control. FAST_LANE, TRUSTED, and STANDARD tiers turn trust history into lower friction, stronger gating, and differentiated pricing.

Buyer objections

Questions before procurement asks them.

Does Valendir replace my agent framework?

No. It is a sidecar clearance layer between agent intent and downstream consequence. Agent frameworks, ERPs, and payment rails remain unchanged.

Does Valendir hold money or documents?

No for initial deployments. Valendir is non-custodial and hash-only: state, artifact hashes, signatures, and release readiness. Your documents and value stay in your systems.

Is this just approval workflow?

No. A clearance is a machine-readable, signed judgment with reason codes and a canonical packet hash. Approvals are one possible input — the state machine also handles mandate checking, bilateral proof matching, dispute freeze, and cryptographic attestation.

Why would legal care?

Because Valendir replaces ambiguous “the AI did it” exposure with a signed, auditable record that the action was authorized, bounded, evidenced, and reviewable by a human — before consequence moved.

What is the enterprise availability story?

Initial deployments run on bbolt (single-node, embedded). Enterprise tier migrates to a replicated store with HA and on-prem options. We discuss architecture tradeoffs openly at pilot scoping — no hidden limitations.

Can Valendir auto-release without human approval?

Yes — for low and medium risk clearances with AUTO_WITHIN_LIMIT mandate policy. High and critical risk always require an explicit human accept action. Risk class and approval threshold are set at clearance creation time by your team, not by Valendir defaults.
